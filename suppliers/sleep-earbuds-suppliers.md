## How to read the prices

AliExpress shows two kinds of price for many of these products:
- **"W" (welcome deal):** a new-shopper price that only applies to a first order. It is **not** a repeatable cost of goods (COGS), so do not use it to model margin. Many of these listings have a regular-price twin listing from the same seller at about $14–17.
- **Regular price:** the repeatable price, used for landed cost below.

"Free shipping" on a US-stock listing means landed cost = unit price, before sales tax. Delivery: AliExpress US "Local+" listings typically quote 3–7 days, and some carry a "Fastest delivery in 4–5 days" tag. **Confirm 2–5 days on a test order.**

**Getting the gifts under target.** Single-unit US dropship listings won't do it. Three options:
1. **Bundle at the source.** Ask the earbud supplier (or its US warehouse) to insert a mask and tape into each parcel. Chinese suppliers often quote about $0.80–1.50 for a mask and $0.40–0.80 for a 30-strip pack at 300–500 units. Combined landed cost is then roughly earbud cost + $1.50–2.50.
2. **Buy bulk into a US 3PL or your own stock**, roughly 300–500 units each, and kit them with the earbuds. This adds a 3PL fee (about $3–4 per order) but keeps a single parcel.
3. **Make the gift digital**, e.g. a night-shift sleep guide, until the bundled supply is ready.

## Supplier outreach checklist (send to each top-5 seller today)

Copy-paste message:

> Hi — I'm launching a sleep earbud offer in the US next week and want to order from your US warehouse. Please confirm:
> 1. US warehouse location (state) and current stock quantity for this SKU
> 2. Delivery time to US customers (business days) and carrier
> 3. FCC ID (or FCC SDoC test report) for this exact model
> 4. MSDS and UN38.3 test summary for the battery (earbud and case)
> 5. Weight of a single earbud (g) and battery life per charge / with case
> 6. Does it have auto-shutoff or a sleep timer? Is touch control on the outer face? (Side sleepers trigger touch controls on the pillow.)
> 7. Can you ship in plain/unbranded packaging? MOQ and price for custom-logo box?
> 8. Can you insert a sleep mask + mouth tape (I can supply or you can source)? Price per unit?
> 9. Sample cost and how fast you can send 2 samples to [your US address]
> 10. Your price for 100 / 300 / 500 units (repeat orders, not new-buyer price)

**Verification steps:**
- Look up the FCC ID they give at **fccid.io** or the FCC Equipment Authorization database. The grantee and photos should match the product.
- The UN38.3 summary should name this battery model and capacity (mAh).
- Place **1 test order to a US address and time it.** That's the only real proof of 2–5 day delivery.
