## Angles that are working (repeated across long-running ads)

1. **Partner snoring** — "Blocks his snoring", "You shouldn't have to sleep on the couch", "I haven't slept in our bed in 8 months" (advertorial). Biggest emotional hook.
2. **Side-sleeper pain** — "Most earbuds hurt after 20–30 min, especially on your side", flat/flush fit, "forget they're in".
3. **Small ears / fit** — ViSound Air "50% smaller", under 3g, stemless.
4. **Auto-shutoff / sleep detection** — "smart enough to turn off when you're out".
5. **Tech/biometrics** — Ozlo sleep insights, For Me Buds HR-adaptive binaural beats.
6. **Substitutes framing earbuds as the problem** — headbands & pillow speakers: "no pressure, nothing stuck in your ears".

## Offer patterns
- BOGO + 60-day trial (VionB) — the volume play.
- Discount codes 10% (Openhagen, Meesue), 60% holiday (VionB).
- HSA/FSA eligibility (Ozlo via Flex) — premium differentiator.
- Kickstarter early-bird scarcity.

## Funnel patterns
- Advertorial "5 reasons" / "I tried X" LP before PDP (VionB everywhere).
- Whitelisted/partnership ads from creator or fake-publication pages ("Sleep Science Weekly", "Dad Finds", "X with Ozlo Sleep").
- Video dominant, 28–48s.

## Gaps / openings
- Mid-price ($60–120) brand between dropship BOGO and Ozlo premium is thin; Raycon just entered.
- Nobody leads with **shift workers / daytime sleepers** in earbud ads (only SnoozeFlex mask does).
- Few ads lean on **travel / plane sleep**, **tinnitus masking**, or **sleep-stage data without a $300 price**.
- Snoring-partner angle is crowded by VionB — need a fresh mechanism or proof (dB test, sleep study) to compete.
